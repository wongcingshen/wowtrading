Reading a signal

Score (−100 to +100)
Positive means buy, negative means sell or avoid. An email is sent when the absolute score reaches the alert threshold on an item that really trades.
Price
Average price of the cheapest 15% of listed units. It ignores a single mispriced listing.
Normal price
The item's own average price over the last 72 hourly snapshots.
Net margin
Gain if you buy at today's price and sell at the normal price, after the auction house cut.
Days of supply
Listed quantity divided by estimated daily sales. Under 1 day means the shelf is nearly empty.
Max units
The smaller of the units listed within 10% of the floor and a quarter of estimated daily sales. Buying more than this makes you the market.

Signal names

Dip
Price at least 2 standard deviations below normal while supply is not flooding, with enough net margin.
Squeeze
Listed quantity far below normal while price is rising.
Spike
Price at least 2 standard deviations above normal. Take profit if you hold it.
Flood
Listed quantity far above normal while price is falling. Avoid.
Cheap vs alternatives
Price unusually low compared with substitute items (same slot, type and level band).
Event
A scheduled event that raises demand for this item category is inside its lead window.

Sales are estimated from listings that disappear between hourly snapshots, so they are a lower bound. Weights and thresholds are starting assumptions until enough history exists to test them.
